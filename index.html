Welcome!

Salam Kids TV is a YouTube channel of gentle 3D cartoons for Muslim children aged 2–7. In every episode Ali, his robot friend Mubi and their friends learn a beautiful Islamic manner — saying Assalamu alaikum, Bismillah and Alhamdulillah, being polite, and being kind to animals.
